芝加哥圣经解释宣言 
 
  
THE CHICAGO STATEMENT ON 
BIBLICAL HERMENEUTICS 
 
国际圣经无误委员会的第一次峰会于 1978 年
10 月 26 至 28 日在芝加哥召开，目的是要重
新确认圣经无误的教义，以期清楚对此教义
的理解以及警告否定此教义的危险。第一次
峰会之后的七年来，神对这项事工的祝福大
大超过了多数的预期。看到诸多有关圣经无
误教义的文学作品，以及日渐增多的对此工
作意义的委身，我们就不住地赞美我们伟大
的神。 
 
 Summit I of the International Council on Biblical 
Inerrancy took place in Chicago on October 26-28, 
1978 for the purpose of affirming afresh the doctrine 
of the inerrancy of Scripture, making clear the 
understanding of it and warning against its denial. In 
the seven years since Summit I, God has blessed that 
effort in ways surpassing most anticipations. A 
gratifying show of helpful literature on the doctrine of 
inerrancy as well as a growing commitment to its value 
give cause to pour forth praise to our great God. 
 
然而，在第一次峰会的任务远未完成的情况
下，我们清楚地看到另一项重要任务也需要
着手处理。我们认识到，相信圣经无误是维
护圣经权威的根基，对此委身的价值就与一
个人对圣经意义的理解一样真实。 
因此，我们需要第二次峰会。在两年的时间
里，我们安排计划并且撰写有关圣经解释的
原则和实践的主题的文章。之后，我们这些
在此文件上签字的人全部参加了在 1982 年 11
月 10 日至 13 日在芝加哥召开的第二次峰
会。 
 
 The work of Summit I had hardly been completed 
when it became evident that there was yet another 
major task to be tackled. While we recognize that 
belief in the inerrancy of Scripture is basic to 
maintaining its authority, the values of that 
commitment are only as real as one's understanding of 
the meaning of Scripture. 
Thus, the need for Summit II. For two years plans were 
laid and papers were written on themes relating to 
hermeneutical principles and practices. The 
culmination of this effort has been a meeting in 
Chicago on November 10-13, 1982 at which we, the 
undersigned, have participated. 
 
与 1978 年的芝加哥宣言的方式相似，我们用
下面的确认与否认的条文来表明我们想澄清
的有关圣经解释中的问题和原则。我们并没
有宣称对此有了完整或系统的处理。但是这
些确认与否认的条文代表了这次会议的大约
一百位与会者和观察家们的共识。参与这样
的讨论，开拓了我们的眼界。为此，我们求
神使用这个辛勤劳动的成果，可以使我们以
及他人可以更加按着正意分解真理的道（提
后二章 15 节）。 
 
 In similar fashion to the Chicago Statement of 1978, 
we herewith present these affirmations and denials as 
an expression of the results of our labors to clarify 
hermeneutical issues and principles. We do not claim 
completeness or systematic treatment of the entire 
subject, but these affirmations and denials represent a 
consensus of the approximately one hundred 
participants and observers gathered at this 
conference. It has been a broadening experience to 
engage in dialogue, and it is our prayer that God will 
use the product of our diligent efforts to enable us and 
others to more correctly handle the word of truth 
(2Tim. 2:15).

ARTICLES OF 
AFFIRMATION AND DENIAL 
 
第一条  Article I 
我们确认：圣经规范性的权威是神自己的权
威，并由教会的主，耶稣基督，所证实。 
我们否认：将基督的权威与圣经的权威分割
开来或将两者对立起来的合理性。 
 
 We affirm that the normative authority of Holy 
Scripture is the authority of God Himself, and is 
attested by Jesus Christ, the Lord of the Church. 
We deny the legitimacy of separating the authority of 
Christ from the authority of Scripture, or of opposing 
the one to the other. 
 
第二条  Article II 
我们确认：正如基督是神也是人有一个位格
一样，圣经是用人的语言表达出来的神的话
语，二者不可分割。 
我们否认：圣经以降卑的人的形式出现就会
有谬误，就如同否认基督降卑为人会有罪一
样。 
 
 We affirm that as Christ is God and Man in one Person, 
so Scripture is, indivisibly, God's Word in human 
language. 
We deny that the humble, human form of Scripture 
entails errancy any more than the humanity of Christ, 
even in His humiliation, entails sin. 
 
第三条  Article III 
我们确认：耶稣基督的位格和工作是整本圣
经的中心焦点。 
我们否认：任何拒绝或者模糊圣经是以耶稣
基督为中心的释经方法是正确的。 
 
 We affirm that the Person and work of Jesus Christ are 
the central focus of the entire Bible. 
We deny that any method of interpretation which 
rejects or obscures the Christ-centeredness of 
Scripture is correct. 
 
第四条  Article IV 
我们确认：曾经默示圣经的圣灵在今日仍然
透过圣经工作，使人生发对圣经的信心。 
我们否认：圣灵会教导人任何与圣经的教训
相抵触的内容。 
 
 We affirm that the Holy Spirit who inspired Scripture 
acts through it today to work faith in its message. 
We deny that the Holy Spirit ever teaches to anyone 
anything which is contrary to the teaching of Scripture. 
 
第五条  Article V 
我们确认：圣灵会使信徒能够掌握圣经并将
圣经应用到他们的生命当中。 
我们否认：不靠着圣灵，属血气的人可以在
灵里分辨圣经信息。 
 
 We affirm that the Holy Spirit enables believers to 
appropriate and apply Scripture to their lives. 
We deny that the natural man is able to discern 
spiritually the biblical message apart from the Holy 
Spirit. 
 
第六条  Article VI 
我们确认：圣经以命题式陈述表达神的真
理。我们宣告：圣经真理既是客观的也是绝
对的。我们进一步确认：如果一个陈述表达

propositional statements, and we declare that biblical 
truth is both objective and absolute. We further affirm

We deny that, while Scripture is able to make us wise

第七条  Article VII 
我们确认：圣经每一句经文所表达的意义都
是单一的、明确的和不能改变的。 
我们否认：承认圣经经文意义的单一性就去
掉了其应用的多样性。 
 
 We affirm that the meaning expressed in each biblical 
text is single, definite and fixed. 
We deny that the recognition of this single meaning 
eliminates the variety of its application. 
 
第八条  Article VIII 
我们确认：圣经包含了一些适用于所有文化
和背景的教训和命令，也包含了一些圣经自
身已经表明只适用在特殊场合的命令。 
我们否认：圣经中的普遍命令和特殊命令间
的区分可以由文化和环境因素决定。我们进
一步否认：普遍命令可以按不同的相关文化
或环境背景来相应处理。 
 
 We affirm that the Bible contains teachings and 
mandates which apply to all cultural and situational 
contexts and other mandates which the Bible itself 
shows apply only to particular situations. 
We deny that the distinction between the universal 
and particular mandates of Scripture can be 
determined by cultural and situational factors. We 
further deny that universal mandates may ever be 
treated as culturally or situationally relative. 
 
第九条  Article IX 
我们确认：释经这个术语，从历史上来讲指
的是解释圣经的原则，也可以适当延伸到包
括一个过程，其中有领会圣经的启示以及如
何与我们的生活相连。  
我们否认：圣经的信息取决于解经者的理
解。因此我们否认：圣经作者和解经者的
“视野”可以确实地“融合”到这样一个地
步，以至于经文传递给解经者的意义最终不
受圣经表达的含义的控制。 
 
 We affirm that the term hermeneutics, which 
historically signified the rules of exegesis, may 
properly be extended to cover all that is involved in 
the process of perceiving what the biblical revelation 
means and how it bears on our lives. 
We deny that the message of Scripture derives from, 
or is dictated by, the interpreter's understanding. Thus 
we deny that the "horizons" of the biblical writer and 
the interpreter may rightly "fuse" in such a way that 
what the text communicates to the interpreter is not 
ultimately controlled by the expressed meaning of the 
Scripture. 
 
第十条  Article X 
我们确认：圣经将神的真理以各种文学语言
方式传递给我们。 
我们否认：人类语言的限制使得圣经不能充
足传递神的信息。 
 
 We affirm that Scripture communicates God's truth to 
us verbally through a wide variety of literary forms. 
We deny that any of the limits of human language 
render Scripture inadequate to convey God's message.

第十二条  Article XII 
我们确认：在圣经翻译时以及在各种文化背
景下教导圣经时，应该只使用那些忠于圣经
教导内容的有同样功能的语言文字。 
我们否认：不顾及跨文化的交流需求或在交
流过程中曲解圣经意义的方法的合理性。 
 
 We affirm that in the task of translating the Bible and 
teaching it in the context of each culture, only those 
functional equivalents which are faithful to the 
content of biblical teaching should be employed. 
We deny the legitimacy of methods which either are 
insensitive to the demands of cross-cultural 
communication or distort biblical meaning in the 
process. 
 
第十三条  Article XIII 
我们确认：熟悉圣经中各种文学类别、形式
和体裁，对于恰当释经是非常重要的；因此
我们看重文体分析，并以其为诸多研经学科
之一。 
我们否认：否定历史性的文学类别可以被正
确地强加于那些呈现事实的圣经叙述之上。 
 
 We affirm that awareness of the literary categories, 
formal and stylistic, of the various parts of Scripture is 
essential for proper exegesis, and hence we value 
genre criticism as one of the many disciplines of 
biblical study. 
We deny that generic categories which negate 
historicity may rightly be imposed on biblical 
narratives which present themselves as factual. 
 
第十四条  Article XIV 
我们确认：圣经中所记载的事件、论述、格
言，虽然以多种合适的文体形式来展现，但
都是符合历史事实的。 
我们否认：圣经中所记载的事件、论述、格
言是圣经作者杜撰的或是从他们身处的传统
习惯来的。 
 
 We affirm that the biblical record of events, discourses 
and sayings, though presented in a variety of 
appropriate literary forms, corresponds to historical 
fact. 
We deny that any event, discourse or saying reported 
in Scripture was invented by the biblical writers or by 
the traditions they incorporated. 
 
第十五条  Article XV 
我们确认：按照圣经的字面或通常的意思来
解经的必须性。字面意思是指文法与历史上
的意思，也就是作者所表达的意义。按照字
面意思来解释要考虑到所有的可在文本中看
到的修辞格式和文体格式。 
我们否认：任何对圣经意义推导却无经文字
义支持的合理性。 
 
 We affirm the necessity of interpreting the Bible 
according to its literal, or normal, sense. The literal 
sense is the grammatical-historical sense, that is, the 
meaning which the writer expressed. Interpretation 
according to the literal sense will take account of all 
figures of speech and literary forms found in the text. 
We deny the legitimacy of any approach to Scripture 
that attributes to it meaning which the literal sense 
does not support. 
 
第十六条  Article XVI 
我们确认：应当使用合理的鉴别方法来确定
正典文本及其含义。 
 We affirm that legitimate critical techniques should be 
used in determining the canonical text and its 
meaning.

我们否认：允许圣经评论的任何方法来质疑
作者所表达的意义，或者是任何圣经教导的
真实性或完整性。 
 
We deny the legitimacy of allowing any method of 
biblical criticism to question the truth or integrity of 
the writer's expressed meaning, or of any other 
scriptural teaching. 
 
第十七条  Article XVII 
我们确认：圣经的合一性、和谐性以及一致
性，并且宣告：圣经自己就是最好的解释
者。 
我们否认：解释圣经时可以试图用某一个段
落修正或反驳另一个段落。我们也否认：圣
经的后期作者在引用或参照早期圣经段落时
会产生错解。 
 
 We affirm the unity, harmony and consistency of 
Scripture and declare that it is its own best interpreter. 
We deny that Scripture may be interpreted in such a 
way as to suggest that one passage corrects or 
militates against another. We deny that later writers of 
Scripture misinterpreted earlier passages of Scripture 
when quoting from or referring to them. 
 
第十八条  Article XVIII 
我们确认：圣经本身对自己的解释总是正确
的，从不会偏离默示内容的单一意义，而是
将其阐明。先知的话语的单一意义包括，但
不局限于：先知对那些话语的理解，并且必
须包含上帝在成就那些话语时所显明的上帝
的意图。 
我们否认：圣经作者们总是明白他们自己所
写词句的全部涵义。 
 
 We affirm that the Bible's own interpretation of itself 
is always correct, never deviating from, but rather 
elucidating, the single meaning of the inspired text. 
The single meaning of a prophet's words includes, but 
is not restricted to, the understanding of those words 
by the prophet and necessarily involves the intention 
of God evidenced in the fulfillment of those words. 
We deny that the writers of Scripture always 
understood the full implications of their own words. 
 
第十九条  Article XIX 
我们确认：解经者对圣经的任何预先理解应
该与圣经教导相和谐并且顺服圣经对其的纠
正。 
我们否认：可以要求圣经适应外来的预先理
解而与其本身不一致，例如，自然主义、进
化主义、科学主义、世俗人文主义和相对主
义。 
 
 We affirm that any preunderstandings which the 
interpreter brings to Scripture should be in harmony 
with scriptural teaching and subject to correction by it. 
We deny that Scripture should be required to fit alien 
preunderstandings, inconsistent with itself; such as 
naturalism, evolutionism, scientism, secular 
humanism, and relativism. 
 
第二十条  Article XX 
我们确认：既然神是一切真理的作者，一切
的真理，包括圣经所记载的以及圣经没有记
载的，都是一致的和紧密的，并且圣经所触
及的有关自然、历史或其它任何领域的事
情，所说的都是真理。我们进一步确认：在
某些个案中，圣经之外的数据对于澄清圣经
的教导以及激发对于错误解释的纠正是有价
值的。 
我们否认：圣经之外的观点可以否定圣经的
教导或比圣经更有优先权。 
 We affirm that since God is the author of all truth, all 
truths, biblical and extrabiblical, are consistent and 
cohere, and that the Bible speaks truth when it 
touches on matters pertaining to nature, history, or 
anything else. We further affirm that in some cases 
extrabiblical data have value for clarifying what 
Scripture teaches, and for prompting correction of 
faulty interpretations. . 
We deny that extrabiblical views ever disprove the 
teaching of Scripture or hold priority over it.

第二十一条  Article XXI 
我们确认：特殊启示与普遍启示是和谐的，
并由此确认圣经的教导与自然事实也是和谐
的。 
我们否认：任何真正的科学事实会与圣经的
某处段落的真实含义不一致。 
 
 We affirm the harmony of special with general 
revelation and therefore of biblical teaching with the 
facts of nature. 
We deny that any genuine scientific facts are 
inconsistent with the true meaning of any passage of 
Scripture. 
 
第二十二条  Article XXII 
我们确认：创世纪第一章到第十一章就如圣
经的其余部分一样，都是属实的记载。 
我们否认：创世纪第一章到第十一章的教导
是神话，并且否认：有关地球历史或人类起
源的科学假设可以被用来推翻圣经关于创造
的教导。 
 
 We affirm that Genesis 1-11 is factual, as is the rest of 
the book. 
We deny that the teachings of Genesis 1-11 are 
mythical and that scientific hypotheses about earth 
history or the origin of humanity may be invoked to 
overthrow what Scripture teaches about creation. 
 
第二十三条  Article XXIII 
我们确认：圣经是清晰明白的，特别是关于
从罪中得拯救的信息。 
我们否认：关于救赎信息，圣经所有的经文
都是同样清晰或具同样分量。 
 
 We affirm the clarity of Scripture and specifically of its 
message about salvation from sin. 
We deny that all passages of Scripture are equally 
clear or have equal bearing on the message of 
redemption. 
 
第二十四条  Article XXIV 
我们确认：一个人对于圣经的理解不是倚靠
圣经学者的专门知识。 
我们否认：一个人可以无视圣经学者的专业
研经成果。 
 
 We affirm that a person is not dependent for 
understanding of Scripture on the expertise of biblical 
scholars. 
We deny that a person should ignore the fruits of the 
technical study of Scripture by biblical scholars. 
 
第二十五条  Article XXV 
我们确认：唯一一种能够充分地传达神的启
示并能适切地运用在生活中的讲道方式，乃
是忠实地把圣经经文当做神的话语来阐释。 
我们否认：除了圣经经文之外，传道人还能
从神那里获得任何其它信息。 
 
 We affirm that the only type of preaching which 
sufficiently conveys the divine revelation and its 
proper application to life is that which faithfully 
expounds the text of Scripture as the Word of God. 
We deny that the preacher has any message from God 
apart from the text of Scripture.

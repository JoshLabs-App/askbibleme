# AskBible Reading Approach

> “But his delight is in the law of the Lord, and on his law he meditates day and night.”
> — Psalm 1:2

> “Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God.”
> — Matthew 4:4

Many people begin reading Scripture to know God more and draw near to Him. Over time, reading easily becomes check-ins, progress tracking, and pressure—falling behind brings guilt; stopping leads to giving up.

We believe the deeper purpose of reading has never been only “finishing the reading,” but **leading people back into a loving relationship with God**.

AskBible offers two main paths for different seasons. Neither is a KPI you must complete—they are companions you can stay with or switch between.

---

## MacArthur’s Reading Logic

John MacArthur stresses that **good books cannot replace reading Scripture itself**. He distinguishes Old and New Testament approaches:

**Old Testament:** Read straight through in book order, about one chapter a day—roughly one pass per year. OT narrative is concrete; questions often clear as you read again and again.

**New Testament:** Needs more time and repetition. In seminary, MacArthur read **all of 1 John every day for thirty days**. Around day seven or eight you may think you “get it”; if you stay through the month, understanding often deepens remarkably.

His core logic:

- **Repeat the same passage until it sinks in**—not skimming, but letting Scripture fill your mind.
- **Segment by book length**—shorter books as one segment, longer books split; finish one segment before the next, in order.
- **Keep one translation for repetition** so wording and location stay in memory; occasionally compare versions for fresh light.
- **Scripture interprets Scripture**—the whole Bible begins to fit together.

MacArthur’s original pattern uses **thirty days per segment** and about **2½ years** per NT round. AskBible shaped that into **Path 2** below. For the full guide (read, interpret, meditate, teach), see [MacArthur’s complete method](/explore/articles/a-macarthur-lifelong-bible-reading).

## Path 1 · Easy Read-Through

Best for: first habits, or a **low-pressure, sustainable** return to Scripture.

### Triple loop

Three **independent** tracks—one short segment each day:

| Track | Daily | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old Testament | 1 chapter | Genesis → Malachi (excluding Wisdom books) |
| New Testament | 1 chapter | Matthew → Revelation |
| Wisdom | 1 psalm / chapter | Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon |

Tracks **do not block each other**. Finish one track, only that track advances; each loops from the start when done.

## Path 2 · Effective Deep Study

Best for: steady readers who want the **NT to sink in**.

### How we adapted MacArthur’s method

- **52 NT stages** in MacArthur’s book order—usually **4–6 chapters** per stage (longer books split). One round = all 52 stages.
- **“N passes” = N days per stage**—not N full rounds through the NT. At **7 days/stage**, you read the same segment for 7 straight days, then move on on day 8.
- **Three depths (pick one)**:
  - **7 days/stage** → about **12 months** for 52 stages
  - **14 days/stage** → about **2 years**
  - **28 days/stage** → about **4 years**
- **OT in parallel:** one chapter per day, read-through order—independent from NT progress.
- **Continuous blocks:** start on the activation day, read the same segment for the chosen number of days, then move on the next day.

Daily order: today’s NT stage first, then one OT chapter.

→ Choose **[NT deep repeat · OT read-through](/read/plans/nt-deep-repeat)** (day 1 starts when you set it active; pick depth first).

## Principles Both Paths Share

AskBible plans are deliberately **not checklists**:

- **No catch-up, no “behind” messaging**—come back anytime without making up missed days.
- **What matters is that you keep returning**, not streaks or completion rates.
- **One segment today is enough**—sometimes one verse outweighs three chapters rushed.
- **No KPI**—spiritual life is not an exam.

---

## Which Path?

| Your season | Suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| Starting out, or need a light load | **Path 1** triple loop |
| Reading steadily, want NT to sink in | **Path 2**, start at **7 days/stage**; deepen to 14 / 28 when ready |
| Either can fit | Build rhythm first, then go deeper—or switch by season |

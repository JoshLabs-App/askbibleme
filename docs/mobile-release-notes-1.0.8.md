# AskBible.me Mobile 1.0.8 (64)

双端统一版本：**1.0.8**，构建号：**64**。

---

## 简体中文（App Store / Google Play）

**本次更新**

- **默认正式研读**：新用户默认 7 天连读计划；首页与读经计划页更清楚说明为何连读、如何读懂圣经。
- **探索 · 轻松读经**：原「读经规划器」改为「轻松读经」；「深度读经」改为「正式研读」，选计划更清晰。
- **会员同步**：登录后可跨设备同步读经进度与计划偏好（需联网）。
- **引导优化**：入门第二步直达轻松读经流程。

---

## English

**What's New**

- **Formal study by default**: New installs default to 7-day repeat reading; home and plans explain why repeating helps you understand Scripture.
- **Explore · Easy reading**: Reading planner renamed for clarity; deep reading is now "Formal study."
- **Member sync**: Sign in to sync reading progress and plan preferences across devices.
- **Onboarding**: Step two opens the easy-reading planner directly.
